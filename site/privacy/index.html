Privacy by absence.

Effective August 27, 2026.

The CLI

MRG has no telemetry. It does not send commands, fixtures, schema information, logs, or reports to Sociobot. It runs locally, starts a disposable container through Docker or Podman, and removes that container after the replay.

This website

The website has no analytics, cookies, accounts, forms, advertising, or third-party runtime scripts. Its service worker stores public site files on your device for offline use. You can clear that cache through your browser.
